Enhancement Suite for Google Maps

🌙 Park Picker

Copy these two values from the Settings page of the Park Picker web app.

Your Google Maps lists

    How to use it
    1. Open one of your lists in Google Maps on this computer. The Park Picker panel appears at the top right.
    2. Press Update all notes in this list. It fills in every place's note and presses Done for you.
    3. The extension remembers each list you open. Next time, Update all lists here does them all.

    For one place: open it, click into its note, press Fill note or Alt+Shift+M, then press Done in Google Maps.
