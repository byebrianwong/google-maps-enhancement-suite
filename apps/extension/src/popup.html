Enhancement Suite for Google Maps

🌙 Park Picker

Copy these two values from the Settings page of the Park Picker web app.

How to use it
  1. Open one of your lists in Google Maps on this computer.
  2. Click a place. The Park Picker panel appears at the top right.
  3. The first time, press Link (or Add) to connect it to your saved place.
  4. Click into the place's note, then press Fill note or Alt+Shift+M.
  5. Press Done in Google Maps to save the note.
